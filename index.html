Services

Clear treatments, explained in plain language.

Ask about a service

Dental Cleaning & Polishing

Removes plaque, tartar, and stains from tooth surfaces to support gum health and fresher breath.

Good for: routine checkups, stains, bleeding gums

Root Canal Treatment

Cleans and seals an infected tooth so the natural tooth can often be saved instead of removed.

Good for: deep decay, severe pain, infected pulp

Dental Fillings

Repairs a decayed or damaged area with tooth-colored or suitable restorative material.

Good for: small cavities, chipped areas, early decay

Crowns & Bridges

Protects weak teeth and replaces missing teeth to restore bite strength, shape, and smile balance.

Good for: post-root-canal teeth, missing teeth

Tooth Extraction

Carefully removes teeth that cannot be restored or may affect surrounding oral health.

Good for: badly damaged teeth, non-restorable teeth

Teeth Whitening

Brightens teeth by reducing stains and discoloration under dental supervision.

Good for: dull smile, stains, special occasions

Children's Dentistry

Supports child hygiene, cavity care, brushing habits, fluoride guidance, and milk-tooth concerns.

Good for: kids, first visits, cavities, habit guidance

Dentures

Replaces missing teeth with removable or fixed options for better chewing, speech, and appearance.

Good for: partial or complete tooth loss

FAQ

Quick answers before you book.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people should book a routine dental checkup every 6 months. If you have active dental issues, the doctor may recommend more frequent visits.

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

Yes, when performed under dental supervision. The doctor checks your oral health first and explains sensitivity care.

Can I walk in?

Appointments are recommended so waiting time is lower and the doctor can plan enough time for your concern.

What happens during the first visit?

Your medical and dental history are reviewed, followed by an oral examination. X-rays may be suggested if needed for diagnosis.

What payment modes are available?

You can pay by UPI apps such as Google Pay or PhonePe, or by cash at the clinic.
